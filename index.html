A ship, a crew, a shared ambition

Space Nomads is an open-ended space RPG where you begin with little more than a ship, a crew and a shared ambition. Explore star systems, hire crew members, trade, fight, complete contracts, and create your own path across a living galaxy.

Become a trader, bounty hunter, explorer, salvager, mercenary, or something in between.

Features

  1. A living galaxy

    NPCs have their own tasks: mining the nearest asteroid cluster, patrolling the system, or fighting each other in faction driven warfare. Events take place at random, and it's your decision to get involved or not.

  2. Procedurally generated

    Everything is generated, from the galaxy itself to the ships. It doesn't tell you how to play, though. It builds the universe and leaves the choices to you.

  3. Choose your crew

    Crew have personality traits and provide buffs for your ship. Find them in space stations or escape pods. They write in their journals, responding to what you do. Targeted an innocent civilian ship? They might have something to say about that.

  4. Build your ship

    Find components and build a sleek fighter, or something that looks like it's about to fall apart. Attach weapons and utility devices, and decide what your ship is capable of.

  5. Make your fortune

    Want to become a miner? A trader? Or just a menace to the galaxy? You decide how to create your fortune and reputation.
